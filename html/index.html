💌 A Letter From My Heart

My Dearest Mamma ❤️,

Happy Birthday to the most special person in my life. 🎂❤️ Today is not just your birthday... it is the day the person who changed my life was born. And honestly, I feel so lucky that life brought you to me. 🥹💕

Mamma, do you remember the day you proposed to me? 🥺❤️ You were the one who took the first step and came into my life with your love. I still feel so special when I think about that moment. You chose me, you trusted me with your heart, and you gave me a beautiful reason to believe in us. 💖

From that moment, you became more than just someone I love. You became my happiness, my comfort, my favorite person, and the person I want to share my little moments with. 🫶🏻❤️

I call you "Mamma" with so much love because that little name has become so special to me. ❤️ Every time I call you Mamma, there is so much love, care and emotion behind those words that I can't even explain properly. 🥹💕

Sometimes I wonder how I got so lucky to have someone like you in my life. Out of all the people in this world, I got you. And that is something I will always be grateful for. 🌎❤️

You have brought so many smiles into my life. Even the smallest conversation with you can make my day better. Your messages, your voice, your silly moments, your care, and even the little things you do without realizing it have become precious to me. 🥰

Mamma, I don't want to lose you. ❤️ I don't want to lose the person with whom I have created so many beautiful memories. I don't want to lose our talks, our laughs, our little fights, our cute moments and everything that makes "us" so special. 🥺💗

I know we won't always have perfect days. There may be misunderstandings, arguments and difficult moments. But even during those moments, I want us to remember why we chose each other in the first place. ❤️

You chose me first, Mamma... ❤️ and I want you to know that choosing you is one of the most beautiful things that has happened in my life. 🥹💍

I don't just want the beautiful moments with you. I want the ordinary days too. I want the random conversations, the silly jokes, the late-night talks, the celebrations, the adventures, the photographs and all the little memories that we haven't created yet. 🫶🏻💕

I want to see you achieve everything you dream about. I want to see you happy, successful and proud of yourself. And whenever you feel tired or life becomes difficult, I want you to remember that I will always be cheering for you. ❤️

Thank you, Mamma, for choosing me. Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for giving me memories that I will always treasure. 🥹❤️

You may never fully understand how much you mean to me, because sometimes even a thousand words are not enough to explain what my heart feels for you. 💕

But if there is one thing I want you to remember forever, it is this:

I am so lucky to have you, Mamma. ❤️🥹

And I don't want to lose you. 🫂❤️

No matter how many birthdays come and go, I hope I am there to wish you again and again. 🎂❤️ I hope we continue creating memories, growing together, supporting each other and choosing each other every day. 🫶🏻

Today, on your birthday, I just want to say:

Happy Birthday, Mamma! 🎂🎉❤️

You are my special person, my happiness, and one of the most beautiful parts of my life. ❤️🌹

Forever yours ❤️
With all my love 🥹💕
Always & Forever ❤️♾️
